# MBT design system
## Direction
A Saudi distribution atlas, framed like a corporate film. Authentic company imagery occupies generous rectangular apertures. Fine bronze routes connect points of the narrative. The website is an established company's identity, not an online shop.
## Palette
The supplied client direction overrides the skill's random palette seed: charcoal `oklch(0.19 0.006 100)`, warm ivory `oklch(0.965 0.012 85)`, bronze `oklch(0.74 0.075 75)`, dark ink `oklch(0.23 0.012 95)`. Preserve the red and green in original MBT logos. Gold occupies less than 10% of the surface.
## Typography
Voice: assured, engraved, international. Manrope for navigation and body, Bodoni Moda for measured English display statements, Noto Sans Arabic for Arabic. Three families, locally stored. Headlines max 96px. No Arabic tracking. No synthetic English/Arabic mirroring.
## Composition
Full viewport opening with cinematic company photograph and a restrained geographical overlay. 72px desktop gutters, max content width 1720px. Alternating broad image fields, typographic intervals, an interactive Saudi network, and editorial image/text rows. No rounded card system. Hairline rules act as wayfinding.
## Motion
Slow image settling, route drawing and deliberate active-state transitions. No scroll hijacking. Animation enhances visible content. Every effect respects reduced-motion preferences. Brand ecosystem uses a static deliberate grid rather than a potentially distracting carousel.
## Components
Integrated header, accessible expanded navigation, language-preserving route switch, underlined directional links, reusable editorial page introduction, source-backed article layouts, category filters with result counts, keyboard-operable map points, email enquiry composer with honest handoff.
## Desktop QA
1920, 1728, 1440, 1366. Basic overflow safety at smaller widths. Desktop first; no dedicated mobile experience in this phase.
