# MBT digital identity
## Register
brand
## Users
International principals evaluating a Saudi distribution partner; Saudi retailers and commercial buyers; candidates and existing customers.
## Product Purpose
Rebuild the company website from verified official content, communicating heritage, distribution infrastructure, and its brand portfolio. Desktop approval is this phase's objective.
## Brand Personality
Established, commercial, precise. A Saudi distribution group with tangible market presence, authentic assets and visible sector breadth.
## Anti-references
WordPress templates, SaaS surfaces, storefronts, repeating card grids, excessive gold, unverified stock logistics photographs.
## Design Principles
- Make the actual distribution network the narrative.
- Preserve source facts and provenance; quarantine contradictions.
- Give authentic imagery room to communicate.
- Arabic has independent content and natural RTL typography.
- Navigation and information discovery remain direct.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, accessible controls, reduced motion, readable contrast, and basic responsive safety. Dedicated mobile design follows desktop approval.
## Decision authority
The detailed user brief supplies the strategic and visual direction and explicitly authorizes professional implementation decisions without routine questions. That takes precedence over skill interview defaults.

## Current acceptance brief
The first desktop visual direction was rejected. RE_AUDIT.md records the second complete source comparison; DESIGN.md defines the substantially rebuilt composition. Existing content and Git history remain intact.
